I work on the control of wound rotor synchronous machines — motors that replace permanent magnets with electromagnets — and on the optimization problems that arise from controlling them. I lift non-convex QCQPs to semidefinite programs to do this. My research sits at the intersection of motor control and mathematical optimization, advised by [Matthias Preindl](https://mplab.ee.columbia.edu/people/matthias-preindl).

Outside the lab, I'm a bartender, a [former competitive saber fencer](https://www.askfred.net/tournaments/18b6c156-b04f-40cb-b3da-83a93588d4e0/results), president of the [Columbia Amateur Radio Club](https://www.w2aee.columbia.edu/), and an experimental sketch comedy performer. I advise the Columbia Formula SAE team and work as a NYSERDA clean energy intern, supporting task forces of New York energy sector expert volunteers.
